The Great Decoupling

Some search pages keep their impressions and quietly lose their clicks. I built an early-warning model for them on FlyRank's production warehouse, with validation grouped by client and a leakage audit.

The hand-written baseline scored 0.00 precision@50. A Random Forest reached 1.93× lift, and its 95% interval [0.00, 0.16] spans zero. The paper reports that plainly, because the honest number is the useful one.

Lessons paid for in reruns

  • A random split is the most expensive way to fool yourself.
  • If your AUC is 0.99, you have not discovered something. You have leaked something.
  • Set the random seed. Then check your row order, because it will humble you.
  • Report the baseline even when it scores zero. Especially then.
