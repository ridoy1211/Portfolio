Lessons paid for in reruns

  • A random split is the most expensive way to fool yourself.
  • If your AUC is 0.99, you have not discovered something. You have leaked something.
  • Set the random seed. Then check your row order, because it will humble you.
  • Report the baseline even when it scores zero. Especially then.
